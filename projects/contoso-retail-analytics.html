Contoso Retail Analytics

Contoso is a fictional global electronics retailer with four years (2021–2024) of sales from stores and an online shop. I built a Power BI report that answers the questions a sales manager would ask, with clear charts and visualizations per question.

The data

How it's organized

The data is set up as a star schema. factSales sits in the center with one row per item sold on an order, and links to three dimension tables through ProductKey, CustomerKey and StoreKey.

  • dimProduct: what was sold (brand, category, color, list price and cost)
  • dimCustomer: who bought it (age, gender, city and country)
  • dimStore: where it sold, including the online store (country, size, open and close dates)

One product, customer or store can appear in many sales. Filters flow from the dimensions into the sales table, so picking a category or country instantly updates every number in the report.

Reflection

What I learned

Start with the question

Each page answers one business question. Starting there kept the report focused instead of becoming a wall of charts.

Know your data

Understanding how the tables connect made it much easier to build accurate visuals and trust the numbers.

Keep it simple

One chart, one takeaway. Clear titles and a few key numbers say more than a crowded dashboard.
