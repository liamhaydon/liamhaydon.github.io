About

I like taking a vague question like "who are our best customers?" and working it down into a query, a number and a recommendation.

I'm from Los Altos, in the San Francisco Bay Area, and I study Information Science with a minor in Business in CU Boulder's College of Media, Design & Information. I plan to graduate in May 2028.

My coursework focuses on how people work with data: designing relational databases and querying them with SQL, turning results into Power BI dashboards, and looking at the ethics of machine learning, including how algorithms can treat people fairly. The business minor connects that work to real decisions.

On campus I'm Social Chair of Pi Kappa Phi, where I manage a $100,000 semester budget, and VP of Operations for the CU Alternative Investment Club. Before college I spent four years as a varsity swimmer and water polo player.
